# SpaceMolt Hunt Skill

Choose wildlife training or harvesting objectives from observed opportunities.
The script selects a fresh individual and owns the complete tactical job.

## When to Use

Choose Hunt for wildlife. Player engagements, guarding, trade and recovery of unrelated
wrecks belong to other stances and do not become authorized through an aggressive mood.

## Prerequisites

Start docked with a supported damage weapon, ammunition, cargo space and reserves.
Read the resolved policy and the common operations guidance; numeric limits come from
execution policy, not historical fights. The current reviewed intelligence applies
only to an observed Phase-Lurker/loadout combination and is not a universal guarantee.

## How to Run

Choose an encounter only from current observations and sufficient reviewed evidence.
Target individual IDs expire during travel; prefer a scouted species and habitat.

## Tool: job__track

Use `job__track` docked to visit bounded wildlife habitats, scan candidates, record
assessments, return and service. Stationless systems are valid destinations for a
sortie; do not manually fly there and expect a docked-start job to become admissible.

## Tool: job__hunt

Use `job__hunt` for one permitted assessed encounter. It refreshes quarry and other
possible opponents, scans, rechecks before initiation, controls the fight, verifies
its result, collects only new victim loot, returns home and services. An avoid or
need-intelligence result is a blocker. A scan alone does not establish enemy weapons.

## Quick Reference

Relaxed never initiates. Cautious requires larger withdrawal margins; Aggressive
allows more time and exposure within permission. Focused and Opportunistic currently
execute the same single objective without diversions. Tired exits and returns.

## Procedure

Compare training and harvesting as separate objectives. Retained loot may be useful
without producing cash; progression requires authoritative skill counters. Ask for
another objective through the planning handoff when evidence blocks the intended one.
Reconsider home when return travel or absent supplies make the job impractical.

## Pitfalls

A victory, an escape and a stalemate differ. Unknown battle participants force the
controller to reconsider; it does not count bystanders as allies. Never reissue a
hunt after a disconnect or after acceptance without a verified battle outcome.

## Verification

Read the battle summary, retreat flag, progression, returned location and service
result. A serviced return does not turn an inconclusive fight into objective success.
